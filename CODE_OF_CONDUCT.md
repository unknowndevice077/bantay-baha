# Code of conduct

Bantay Baha exists to keep people safe. The people who work on it should be treated the same way.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to code, electronics or flood response.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Say what you think is wrong and why.
- Give credit. Say who found the bug, wrote the fix or drew the diagram.
- Assume people mean well, and ask when you aren't sure.
- Keep it safe to be wrong. Everyone here has shipped a bug.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in any language.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or comments.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Pressuring someone to work on something, or to hurry, when they've said no.

## Safety claims are a special case

This project warns people about floods. Please don't overstate what it can do, in issues, pull requests or docs. If something hasn't been tested on a real creek, say so. [Limitations and next steps](docs/limitations-and-next-steps.md) is the place to be honest about gaps.

## Enforcement

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, issues and commits that break these rules, and can block anyone who keeps breaking them. To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository, or contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept private.

This is a short version, inspired by the Contributor Covenant (<https://www.contributor-covenant.org>), which applies wherever this one is silent.
