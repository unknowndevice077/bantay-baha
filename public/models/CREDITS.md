# Character models

All seven characters are by **Quaternius** (https://quaternius.com), from the *Ultimate Modular Men* and
*Ultimate Modular Women* packs, released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain). Downloaded from Poly Pizza:

- https://poly.pizza/bundle/Ultimate-Modular-Men-Pack-ZiH8muWqwQ: casual-man, beach-man, hoodie-man, farmer, worker
- https://poly.pizza/bundle/Ultimate-Modular-Women-Pack-aCBDXDdTNN: woman-dress, woman-casual

Changes for this project: kept only the Idle, Walk, Run, Wave and Interact animations, and compressed the
meshes with meshopt (`EXT_meshopt_compression`). Skin and hair colours are tinted at runtime.
