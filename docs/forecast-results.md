# Forecast results

Generated by `python tools/forecast/evaluate.py` on 2026-10-12. Re-run `generate_dataset.py`, `train.py` and `evaluate.py` to reproduce (all seeded).

## What is predicted

The water level 30 and 60 minutes ahead, from the last 30 minutes of sensor readings and rainfall (features: `level_m`, `excess_m`, `d5_m`, `d10_m`, `d20_m`, `d30_m`, `rain_mmhr`, `rain10_mmhr`, `rain30_mmhr`, `rain_trend_mmhr`). The forecaster does **not** know future rainfall, which is where most of the remaining error comes from.

## Data

- 900 synthetic storms (seed 2026) run through the simulator's own catchment model (`src/sim.ts`: linear reservoir, 10 s steps), sampled each minute, with the sensor's ±3 mm jitter.
- Profiles: the Auto-Storm typhoon band (exact, and time/intensity-rescaled), two-band storms, flash floods, long drizzles; gusty rain wobble; wet or dry starting creek.
- Split by storm, never by minute: 540 train, 180 validation (model selection), 180 test (this report). 52,632 test samples.

## Accuracy on held-out storms

MAE = mean absolute error of the predicted level, in cm. *Rising* = samples where the creek actually rose more than 10 cm in the next 30 min. *High water* = samples already at ADVISORY or above. *Status* = predicted NORMAL / ADVISORY / WARNING / EVACUATE matches the true status at that time.

| Method | MAE +30 | MAE +60 | MAE +30 rising | MAE +60 rising | MAE +30 high water | MAE +60 high water | Status +30 | Status +60 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Persistence (no change) | 38.6 cm | 52.4 cm | 92.4 cm | 100.8 cm | 100.5 cm | 145.9 cm | 77.1 % | 74.2 % |
| Trend (last 10 min continues) | 50.0 cm | 110.1 cm | 85.7 cm | 182.2 cm | 147.6 cm | 324.2 cm | 80.9 % | 74.9 % |
| Ridge regression | 31.2 cm | 43.5 cm | 71.7 cm | 87.9 cm | 47.3 cm | 47.1 cm | 84.2 % | 86.0 % |
| **Exported model (gbr)** | 20.2 cm | 30.1 cm | 52.0 cm | 73.7 cm | 39.6 cm | 39.6 cm | 86.3 % | 85.1 % |

The exported model cuts the error of the naive persistence forecast by 48 % at +30 min and 43 % at +60 min.

## Warning lead time

For each test storm that reached WARNING (280 cm): how long before the actual crossing the forecast first said "WARNING within the next 60 minutes".

- Storms reaching WARNING: 109; median lead time **18 min** (25th percentile 13 min, 75th 24 min).
- Storms that never reached WARNING: 71; the forecast predicted WARNING at some point in 24 of them (34 % false-alarm rate).

## The simulator's Auto-Storm

On the exact storm the simulator plays (storm 0, a training storm, so optimistic): MAE 19.9 cm at +30 min and 21.1 cm at +60 min, vs 60.8 / 69.7 cm for persistence.

## Limits

- Synthetic data from the same model that drives the simulator: real creeks have backwater, debris jams, upstream releases and rating changes this does not capture. Retrain on recorded sensor data before relying on it.
- No rainfall forecast is used. Feeding PAGASA nowcasts would reduce the +60 min error most.
- See [failure-modes.md](failure-modes.md) for how the app behaves when the forecast is wrong or unavailable.
